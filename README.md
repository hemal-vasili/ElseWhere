 ## ElseWhere

> **Your little world. Together.**

ElseWhere is a private, romantic digital space designed for couples to **play together, save memories, build their story, and create a world that feels like their own**.

The project is being developed as a modern, immersive website with a premium UI/UX and a focus on meaningful shared experiences.

---

## ✨ What is ElseWhere?

ElseWhere brings different parts of a relationship into one beautiful space.

### Core areas

- 🎮 **Together** — Play games and shared activities.
- 📸 **Memories** — Save and revisit meaningful moments.
- 🌍 **Our World** — Build and customize a shared digital world.
- 🏠 **Hub** — A central space connecting everything.
- ⚙️ **Settings** — Manage personal and experience preferences.

---

## 🎨 Design Direction

ElseWhere is designed around a:

- Premium, romantic visual identity
- Dark and warm atmosphere
- Smooth, lightweight animations
- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts
- Clean and immersive user experience
- Accessible and user-friendly interactions

The goal is to make ElseWhere feel less like a traditional website and more like **a private little world for two people**.

---

## 🛠️ Technology

The frontend is built using:

- **HTML5**
- **CSS3**
- **JavaScript**
- **Supabase** for backend/database services where required

The project intentionally keeps the frontend lightweight and avoids unnecessary frameworks.
